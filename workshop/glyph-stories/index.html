GLYPH STORIES

Fetches random stories to read and keep. Lock your Nothing Phone (4a) Pro and shake it: the story scrolls right to left across the 13×13 Glyph Matrix on the back. Shake again to stop — next time it picks up 10 words before where you stopped.

IF THE MATRIX STAYS DARK

Turn on Nothing's Glyph developer mode (lasts 48 hours):

adb shell settings put global nt_glyph_interface_debug_enable 1

USING IT

  1. Open the app. It fetches a first story; FETCH A RANDOM STORY gets more. Tap one in the library to make it current.
  2. Set READING SPEED, then turn SHAKE READER on.
  3. Lock the phone and shake it: two firm shakes. A short buzz means it started, a double buzz means it stopped.
  4. PLAY ON GLYPH NOW tries it without locking.
